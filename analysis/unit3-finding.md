# Unit 3 Finding: Rider No-Shows and the Frozen Driver Bonus

**To:** Dashboard team
**From:** Trust and Data team

The dashboard said about half of all trips end in a rider no-show, and because of that, the quarterly driver bonus payout got frozen. We went through the trip records, and that's not what's in there. Out of 200 trips, 156 finished normally, 34 were cancelled, and 10 are in dispute. Only 12 were rider no-shows, which works out to 6%, or roughly 1 in 17. Even if you counted every trip that didn't finish normally, that's 34 cancellations plus 10 disputes, or 44 of 200 trips, which is 22%. That's still nowhere near half. The driver's concern about the dashboard was justified.

We don't have the dashboard's actual calculation, so we can't say exactly how it landed on "about half." What we can tell you is the kind of mistake that would do it, because we reproduced it. Every completed and disputed trip has no cancellation reason on file, and no cancelled trip is missing one. If a calculation says "count the trips whose reason is not Rider no show," the database quietly skips every trip with no reason at all. In our test, that rule should have counted the 188 trips that weren't rider no-shows, but it counted only 22, because 166 trips with no reason just vanished, with no error and no warning. A rate worked out from a group that lopsided can come out way off while the report still runs fine and looks believable, which is probably why nobody caught it. We'd ask your team to check whether the dashboard's calculation does this. We also tested a second common mistake, where a report tries to use a label before it's been created. That one throws an error right away, so it wouldn't have produced a believable wrong number.

On the payout, the numbers don't back up a freeze based on the "half" figure. All 12 no-show trips have a $0 fare, and they're spread across 9 of our 15 drivers, with nobody having more than 3, so no driver stands out. We don't know what failure rate the payout rule actually requires, so our recommendation is to fix the dashboard and re-check it against these counts, then lift the freeze if the corrected number is within the rule. The team may also want to consider not counting rider no-shows against drivers, since the record says the rider didn't show up. The 10 disputed trips deserve their own look in a separate investigation.
