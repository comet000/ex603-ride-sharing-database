# Unit 4 Finding: The Real No-Show Rate, and Who's Behind It

**To:** Leadership team
**From:** Trust and Data team

**Bottom line:** About 1 in 17 trips ends in a rider no-show (6.0%), not the "about half" the old dashboard showed. Most are spread thinly across drivers, but we'd review trips involving driver 2 first, then driver 3; driver 4 needs more trip history.

## The real rate

Out of 200 trips, 12 were rider no-shows. That's 6.0%, which confirms what we reported in Unit 3.

## Which drivers have recorded no-shows

For most drivers, a no-show is rare. Of the 13 drivers who had trips, four (drivers 5, 8, 10, and 13) had none, and seven had exactly one. Three drivers stand out:

| Driver | No-shows | Trips | No-show rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | 3 | 16 | 18.8% |
| 4 | 1 | 6 | 16.7% |
| 3 | 2 | 13 | 15.4% |

Together, these three drivers had half of all no-shows (6 of 12) on under a fifth of the trips (35 of 200).

## Where it happens

The North has the highest rate at 7.8% (6 of 77 trips), then Central at 6.0% (5 of 83), then the South at 2.5% (1 of 40). By city, Hue is highest at 10.0% (4 of 40 trips) and Danang is lowest at 2.3% (1 of 43), even though both are in Central. These area numbers are built on very few no-shows, so we wouldn't treat the gaps as firm.

## Who we'd investigate

1. **Driver 2, first.** Our busier drivers average about a 4.8% no-show rate, which corresponds to about 0.77 no-shows at driver 2's trip count, versus three recorded. This is a benchmark, not a prediction. Their three were spread across two regions, so it isn't one location.
2. **Driver 3, second.** The rate is high on a modest number of trips, so this calls for a closer look, not a conclusion.
3. **Driver 4, watch only.** The 16.7% comes from a single no-show on six trips. That's too little to act on, but worth checking again after more trips.

## What this doesn't tell us

A recorded rider no-show does not establish that the driver caused the event. Driver ratings show no simple relationship to the recorded no-show rates. The three drivers above are rated 4.94, 4.88, and 4.91, while the two lowest-rated drivers with trips (3.65) each had just one no-show. With only 12 no-shows in total, one or two trips can swing a driver's rate a lot, so we'd treat this as a list of where to look, not grounds for any penalty.

## Suggested next step

Review the individual trips for drivers 2 and 3 to see what happened, and revisit driver 4 once they have more history.
